# Weekly March 15, 2026: COVID, sewing, Dungeon Club, Void AM 1680

Been kind of out of it the last two weeks due to COVID. I think the most frustrating aspect has been another cognitive discontinuity on top of two weeks of discontinuities. It has made it very difficult to get my head into a new project at work. 

Unpacked the sewing machine and finally got my first cuts and stitches on a [Free Sewing Simone](https://freesewing.eu/showcase/simone-by-gaelle/
). See [earlier post for background.](https://kaes-blog.bearblog.dev/why-i-do-queer-diy-fashion/). My hope is to complete a torso mockup by the end of the weekend so that I can assess whether I need to make pattern adjustments. The torso is the most critical fitting point. 

One of the things I like about Bear Blog style is the absence of an obligatory banner image. 

Got to read Lee Ostertag's *Dungeon Club, volume 2*. It is a nice little younger reader graphic novel about teens using Dungeons and Dragons for self-expression. Looking forward to *Die Loaded* in July (trade paperback release) for a more adult exploration of the topic. Currently reading Vonda McIntyre's *Starfarers.*

Did my first play through of [Void AM 1680](https://bannerlessgames.itch.io/void-1680-am).  The game asks you to take the role of an AM radio DJ and progresses by creating a playlist and recorded responses to callers through random prompts. It's a nice relaxing solo TTRPG that can be played while sick using my phone. My DJ persona was Time Traveler Radio, calling out from somewhere to somewhen.
